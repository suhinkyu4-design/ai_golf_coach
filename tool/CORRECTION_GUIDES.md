# Correction guide UI

Seven guides are rendered as original vector diagrams with three short practice cues. The diagrams are illustrative, not measured poses or a reconstruction of the user. Left-handed display mirrors the illustration. Only rule_classified + a recognized warning/fault grade receives corrective guidance; within_rule receives maintenance copy, missing evidence receives no corrective prescription.

The five body-motion guides follow the selected motion in MotionVisualCard. Casting and over-the-top guidance is inside the existing detailed rule cards. Replaced the separate fixed-priority practice paragraph to prevent conflicting guidance after selecting another motion.

Definitions consulted (2026-09-22):
- https://www.mytpi.com/improve-my-game/swing-characteristics/early-extension
- https://www.mytpi.com/improve-my-game/swing-characteristics/sway
- https://www.mytpi.com/improve-my-game/swing-characteristics/chicken-winging
- https://www.mytpi.com/improve-my-game/swing-characteristics/loss-of-posture

These sources describe swing characteristics; they do not validate this app's 2D thresholds, head-motion proxy, illustrations, individual diagnosis or five-repetition practice suggestion. The app's slow, ball-free practice cues are editorial guidance, not copied TPI drill instructions. No external illustrations are copied.
