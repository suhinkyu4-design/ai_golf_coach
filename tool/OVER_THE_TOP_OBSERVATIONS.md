Pairs use temporally supported full-ray shaft candidates at matched grip heights (difference <= 6% of current projected torso length). Sample and candidate coordinate dimensions must match; duplicate decoded images and invalid geometry are excluded. Back and down images retain the same video rotation correction. The comparison endpoint is limited to a common observed shaft length, capped at 75% of torso length, not a detected club head.

The optional Analysis Details screen opens paired images with grip-height guides, the observed candidate ray, timestamps, image-plane direction difference, and (when ball-side direction is available) normalized signed outward displacement. Narrow screens stack the pair; wide screens place them side by side. Frame decode failures and orientation/dimension mismatches do not display a misleading overlay. Temporary images are deleted on change/dispose, including stale asynchronous requests.

## Automatic rear-view screening (v7; supersedes earlier disabled-only status)
The matched-height diagnostic pairs remain separate from a new shaft-reference rule. TPI describes observing the downswing relative to a backswing shaft slot in target-line footage: https://www.mytpi.com/improve-my-game/swing-characteristics/over-the-top . This implementation is an APP HEURISTIC inspired by that geometry, not the complete TPI protocol, verified clubhead tracking, or a validated coaching diagnosis. No numerical thresholds were taken from that source.

Evidence: three continuous, rising backswing lines with distal image support establish an upper shaft reference; three continuous descending observations covering at least0.2 projected torso length establish downswing evidence. Duplicate images, unknown direction, unsupported view, nonfinite/out-of-frame geometry, dimension mismatch, ambiguous shafts and insufficient observations yield unavailable, never normal.
